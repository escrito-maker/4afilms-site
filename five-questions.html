Five Questions

In Five Questions, Jill comes to terms with an early trauma in her life. In each of the film's five scenes, a question is asked that propels Jill forward and toward an answer that opens a path to resolution.

Director's Statement

It struck me how many questions are asked of us on a daily basis, and how few we actually answer.

David Arrow made Five Questions as a creative challenge: to let questions drive the story. Sometimes a question suggests its own answer, and sometimes the response is a surprise. Across five compact scenes, dialogue and image carry Jill toward resolution.

— David Arrow, Writer / Director
